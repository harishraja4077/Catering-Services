Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we receive most often from our clients.

How do I get a catering quote? +

Simply fill out our contact form, call us, or email us with your event details including date, number of guests, and type of event. We'll provide a detailed quote within 24 hours.

Is there a tasting session available? +

Yes! We offer tasting sessions for our Gold and Platinum packages. You can sample selected menu items before making your final decision.

What is your cancellation policy? +

We have a flexible cancellation policy. You can cancel or reschedule your event up to 7 days before the event date for a full refund of your deposit.

Do you offer delivery and setup? +

Absolutely! All packages include professional delivery, setup, and cleanup services. Our team will handle all the logistics so you can focus on enjoying your event.

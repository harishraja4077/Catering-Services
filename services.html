FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our catering services and processes.

How far in advance should I book? +

We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance for weddings and large events, and 2-4 weeks for smaller gatherings. During peak season (May-October), we suggest booking even earlier.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions? +

Absolutely! We can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, kosher, and other dietary needs. Our chefs create delicious alternatives to ensure every guest is catered for.

Do you provide setup and event staff? +

Yes, all of our packages include professional setup and cleanup. Depending on your package, we also provide trained service staff including waiters, bartenders, and event coordinators.

Can I customize my menu? +

Of course! All menus are customizable. During your consultation, we'll work together to create a menu that reflects your tastes, theme, and budget.

What is the minimum order size? +

Our minimum order is 10 guests for most services, and 25 guests for plated dinner service. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.

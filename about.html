Our Story

From A Small Kitchen to Culinary Excellence

Stackly Catering began in 2011 with a simple dream — to create exceptional food experiences that bring people together. What started as a small family business in a modest kitchen has grown into one of the most trusted catering companies in the region.

Over the years, we've catered for 2,500+ events, from intimate dinners for ten to grand celebrations for a thousand guests. Our passion for food and commitment to quality has earned us a reputation for perfection.

Today, our team of over 50 professionals — including award-winning chefs, skilled event planners, and dedicated service staff — works tirelessly to craft experiences that exceed expectations.
